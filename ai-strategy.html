04  How we'd win

Four shifts the whole product follows

From verdicts to evidence: lead with what was found and where, not with a judgement.

From AI destinations to contextual help: stop sending people to an AI place; put it where the work already is.

From false certainty to calibrated confidence: say how sure the system is, in terms a reviewer can act on.

From model runs to human behaviour: measure what people did with the output, not what the model produced.

05  Evidence before verdicts

A verdict a reviewer can't check is a verdict they won't use

[What changed in the output, and what it cost to produce the evidence alongside the finding.]

06  Measurement

We were counting the model's activity, not the human's

What we had: runs and generated rows.

What we needed: opened → read → verified → accepted or dismissed → returned.
