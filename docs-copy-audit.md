### Triggered by `/activation|onboarding|setup|getting started|template|37%/i`

> Part3 grew through a land-and-expand sales model supported by white-glove customer onboarding. As the customer base expanded, relying on CX to get every firm up and running became difficult to scale. I took ownership of activation across product strategy and design, leading the initiative alongside a Customer Success Manager. I helped set growth priorities, designed onboarding improvements, and defined how we would measure progress. {2}


### Triggered by `/\\bai\\b|data|reporting|dashboard|analytics|chart|insights/i`

> Architects use Part3 to manage construction administration, from reviewing submittals and responding to RFIs to tracking changes and drawing revisions. These workflows generate valuable information about project progress, turnaround times, and outstanding work. But while Part3 helped teams manage their work, customers had limited control over how they could analyze and report on it. I identified an opportunity to turn that data into more useful insights, defining a customizable dashboard experience with AI-assisted reporting. {3}
