05  How we'd win

Four rules the redesign follows

One entry point: a reviewer should not need to know how the project was configured.

One surface: document, markup and response in the same place.

Role-shaped: same record, shaped to what each role owes the next one.

Separate the jobs: responding and responding on behalf are different acts.
